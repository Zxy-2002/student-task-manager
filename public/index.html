    Feature flags (admin)

    A feature appears only when both on is checked and rollout is greater than 0%. 100% enables it for all users. 0% enables it for nobody, even when on is checked. A percentage in between enables it for that share of users. Turning on off disables it for everyone, regardless of the rollout percentage.
